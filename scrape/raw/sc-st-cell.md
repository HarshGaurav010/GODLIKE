# SC/ST Cell

SC/ST Cell for employees of IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

The IIT (ISM) Dhanbad perpetually endeavors to ensure a very cordial and conducive atmosphere in the Institute where all the segments of Faculty/Officers/Staff members (including contractual/ casual/ temporary etc.) can discharge their duties without any sorts of discriminations concerning caste/creed/race/region/religion/sex or place of birth; and also without any forms and manifestations of discriminations of any other kinds. This Institute has Liaison Officer for SC/ST, and also an Equal Opportunity Cell in place with a view to implement the Government’s reservation schemes/ various other welfare schemes in the Institute in true letter and spirit.

Vision

To be an institution of excellence in higher education that continually responds to the changing social realities through the development and application of knowledge, towards creating a people- centered and ecologically sustainable society that promotes and protects the dignity, equality, social justice and human rights for all, with special emphasis on marginalized and vulnerable groups.

Mission

In pursuance of its vision and guiding principles, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad organizes teaching programs to facilitate the development of competent and committed professionals for practice, research and teaching; undertakes research; develops and disseminates knowledge; and reaches out to the larger community through extension, at the local, national, regional and international levels.

Purpose

To look into the complaints/allegations related to discriminations against the SC/ST employees of this Institute.

Terms and Reference of the Advisory Committee

- To act as an inquiry authority on individual complaints pertaining to discriminations and related allegations arising at the Institute regarding SC/ST category.

- To ensure that victims and witnesses are not victimized or discriminated owing to their complaints/allegations.

- The Committee shall schedule and conduct meeting as per their convenience.

- The Committee shall submit its report containing findings and recommendations to the Director in a confidential cover.

- The Committee should meet twice a year to review the positions and to solve the problems relating to the SC/ST employees.

- The tenure of this Committee will continue until further orders.
