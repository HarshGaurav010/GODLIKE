## Additional Information

Foreign / NRI / PIO candidates are requested to contact Dean (International Relations and Alumni Affairs) via email: diraa@iitism.ac.in.

Anti-ragging affidavit needs to be submitted by the candidates and their parents/guardians.

Fill the online affidavit form available at:

- [Anti Ragging Website](http://www.antiragging.in/Site/Affidavits_Registration.aspx)

- [Aman Movement Website](http://www.amanmovement.org/)

Once the form is filled successfully, students will receive the undertakings via email. Print out and sign both the Student's Anti-Ragging Undertaking and the Parent's Anti-Ragging Undertaking to submit during admission.

A urine-based testing report is required as an add-on component to the Medical certificate for students reporting for admission. Urine-based testing will not have any academic, administrative, residential or disciplinary consequences in terms of punishment, suspension, expulsion, denial of admission or hostel facilities.
