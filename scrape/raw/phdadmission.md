# Ph.D. Admission

Candidates can apply in the Ph.D. program throughout the year as per their eligibility and choice. However, applications received under a particular mode of Ph.D. admission is not transferrable to another mode of Ph.D. admission.

#### Ph.D. (Regular)

###### with Institute Assistantship

IIT(ISM), Dhanbad provides Institute Assistantship (IA) to the deserving and eligible candidates qualified through the institute selection procedure and, thus, providing ample opportunities for carrying out research leading to the full-time Ph.D. degree.

#### Ph.D. (Externally Funded)

###### with DST-INSPIRE, CSIR/UGC-JRF or Any other National level fellowship

Candidates with External Funded Fellowships such as DST-INSPIRE, CSIR/UGC-JRF or any other National level fellowship can apply throughout the year through this portal.

#### Ph.D. (Part Time)

###### For Working Professionals

Candidates such as Working Professionals with a minimum of 2 years of experience may apply for Ph.D from this portal.
