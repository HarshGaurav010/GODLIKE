About Us

Welcome to the Office of the Dean Students’ Welfare (DSW). Being a fully residential campus, DSW of IIT(ISM) takes care of day to day life of a student with support from all corners of the institute. Besides, academic programmes campus life provides an ideal setting for students to participate in recreational and extracurricular activities.

Preamble

The Office of the Dean (Students’ Welfare) at IIT(ISM) Dhanbad is dedicated to enriching student life and supporting the overall well-being of every student on campus. We act as a central hub for student engagement, connecting students with academic support, mental health and wellness services, cultural and sports activities and various student-led initiatives. Our mission is to foster a positive and inclusive campus environment where students can explore their interests, overcome challenges, and grow both personally and professionally. From resolving concerns to celebrating achievements, we’re here to ensure that every student feels heard, valued, and empowered throughout their journey at IIT(ISM). By encouraging active participation, collaboration, and innovation, we aim to create a vibrant student community that contributes to the sustainable growth and dynamic spirit of the institute.

Vision

To create a vibrant and supportive environment where every student at IIT(ISM) Dhanbad can grow academically, personally, and socially. We envision a campus where knowledge is freely shared, resources are accessible to all, and values like discipline, empathy, and collaboration guide student life. By nurturing creativity, resilience, and a spirit of community, we aim to empower students to become well-trained individuals, future leaders and technocrats ready to make a positive impact in the world and society.

Mission

To support the sustainable and inclusive growth of every student at IIT(ISM) Dhanbad by expanding opportunities, fostering a nurturing environment, and encouraging all- round development. We are committed to helping students excel academically, grow personally, engage socially, and lead responsibly — by providing access to the right resources, mentorship, and platforms that empower them to reach their full potential.

Objectives

- To cultivate an inclusive and supportive environment that promotes the all-round development of students — academically, emotionally, and socially.

- To encourage a culture of self-discipline, responsibility, and mutual respect as a foundation for personal and academic growth.

- To assist students by effectively implementing various support schemes and scholarships offered by the Institute, as well as State and Central Governments.

- To enhance hostel life and residential facilities, fostering a safe, comfortable, and community-oriented atmosphere — a true “home away from home.”

- To provide students with opportunities to engage in real-world problem-solving through academic, cultural, social, and technical frameworks.

- To organize and support diverse student-led activities — including cultural, sports, and technical events — that help discover and nurture individual talents beyond the classroom.

- To promote sustainability and environmental awareness by maintaining a clean, green, and eco-conscious campus.
