## Director’s Message

I believe profoundly in the strength and promise of IIT(ISM) Dhanbad. It is curiosity that fosters the growth of a community, making it unique. Curiosity and accuracy are essential for knowledge, wisdom, intelligence, and stability; therefore, let this methodical and comprehensive way of thinking serve as a guide and inspiration for our endeavours.

Together, let us embrace the future with curiosity and determination. I look forward to witnessing the remarkable contributions our community will make to society.

“उत्तिष्ठत जाग्रत प्राप्य वरान्निबोधत ।”

( Arise, awake, and stop not till the goal is reached!)

### Sensor Breakthrough

Prof. J. Nayak and his team have published a study demonstrating highly sensitive room-temperature acetic acid gas sensing using two-dimensional graphitic carbon nitride, advancing the development of efficient and selective gas sensor technologies.

[Read\\
More ![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/files/arrow.png)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10854-026-16898-6)

![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/files/alloy-innovation.jpg)

### Alloy Innovation

Prof. M. R. Rahul and his team have published a study demonstrating simulation-guided additive manufacturing of Fe–Ni–Nb medium entropy alloys, paving the way for predictive alloy design and controlled in-situ alloying through laser powder bed fusion.

[Read\\
More ![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/files/arrow.png)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40964-026-01564-4)

![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/files/crustal-insight.jpg)

### Crustal Insights

Dr. Dhiraj Kumar Singh’s latest research unveils the crustal structure and tectonic evolution of Northeast India, offering new insights into the Shillong Massif, Dauki Fault, and Indo-Burma subduction system.

[Read\\
More ![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/files/arrow.png)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2026JSeis..30...21S/abstract)

![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/files/parkinsons-insight.jpg)

### Parkinson's Insight

Prof. Niladri Patra and his team publish a study in Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling revealing key conformational mechanisms of the Parkinson’s disease-linked kinase LRRK2 through advanced computational simulations.

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad’s centenary lecture explores Swami Vivekananda’s message of youth, purpose, discipline and responsible use of science and technology for nation-building. [![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/images/solar_round-arrow-right-bold.png)](https://www.iitism.ac.in/#)

![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/NewsIcon/News_6abb9aed16980.jpeg)Global Connections

IIT (ISM) Dhanbad’s international students, led by Prof. Rajni Singh, Dean (International Relations), interacted with the Hon’ble Governor of Jharkhand, Shri Santosh Kumar Gangwar, highlighting global learning, cultural exchange and India’s growing international academic engagement. [![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/images/solar_round-arrow-right-bold.png)](https://www.iitism.ac.in/#)

![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/NewsIcon/News_6abb9ab5d7cc2.jpeg)Groundwater Futures

INGWC-2026 brings groundwater experts, researchers and stakeholders together to address climate-linked challenges and promote scientific, sustainable and community-based water management. [![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/images/solar_round-arrow-right-bold.png)](https://www.iitism.ac.in/#)

![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/storage/NewsIcon/News_6abb9a8bac76d.jpeg)Smarter Mining

KHANAN’26 brings students, academia and industry together to explore safer, smarter, sustainable and technology-driven mining practices. [![](https://www.iitism.ac.in/images/solar_round-arrow-right-bold.png)](https://www.iitism.ac.in/#)
